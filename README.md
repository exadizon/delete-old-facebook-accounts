# How to Delete Old Facebook Accounts

A step-by-step guide to removing a Facebook account you no longer have access to using Facebook's identity verification process.

---

## Overview

If you have an old Facebook account that you can't access — or you want to remove an account that was created using your identity — you can report it to Facebook as an impersonation and request its deletion by submitting a government-issued ID. Facebook will then review and delete the account.

---

## Steps

### 1. Go to the Facebook Help Form

Open the following link in your browser:

**[https://m.facebook.com/help/contact/295309487309948](https://m.facebook.com/help/contact/295309487309948)**

> This is Facebook's official form for reporting accounts that are impersonating you.

---

### 2. Answer "No" When Asked If You Have a Facebook Account

When the form asks **"Do you have a Facebook account?"**, select **No** — even if you currently have one.

> This is the key step. By saying "No," you are telling Facebook that someone else created an account using your identity without your knowledge or permission. This triggers their impersonation removal process.

---

### 3. Upload a Government-Issued ID

You will be asked to upload a photo ID to verify your identity. Accepted forms of ID typically include:

- Driver's licence
- Passport
- National identity card
- Birth certificate (in some regions)

> **Tip:** If the upload is not accepted on the first try, keep attempting — the form can be finicky. It will work eventually.

---

### 4. Submit the Form

Once your ID is uploaded, submit the form. Facebook will review your report and delete the account associated with your identity.

---

## Why This Works

By going through this flow, you are effectively telling Facebook:

> *"I do not have a Facebook account, but someone is using my identity on the platform."*

Facebook then treats the account as an impersonation of a real person (you), verifies your identity via the uploaded ID, and removes the fraudulent account.

---

## Notes

- This process works for accounts you created years ago and no longer have login access to.
- It also works if someone else created an account using your name/photos.
- Facebook typically processes these requests within a few days, though it can sometimes take longer.
- You will not need to log in to any Facebook account to complete this process.

---

## Disclaimer

This guide is provided for informational purposes only. Always ensure that any ID you upload is submitted directly to Facebook through their official website. Do not share your ID with any third-party site or individual.
